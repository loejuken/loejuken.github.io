Background & Journey

About Me

I was born and grew up in Phoenix, Arizona, where I quickly found my love and dedication for swimming, as well as an interest in current electronics that stemmed from my Dad’s new gadgets. I started swimming at 6 years old, and ever since it has been a key piece of my life. In High school, I spent upwards of 30 hours a week dedicated to swimming. It brought me my best friends, extreme resilience, and many learning opportunities. Later in that time, I even took up a captain role where I mentored over 10 swimmers and worked them through swimming technique as well as the complicated mental behind the sport. As a result, I learned how to teach others and grew even closer to swimming.

Away from swimming, my love for technology also continued to grow. With my brother, I became increasingly interested in PCs for gaming. We really wanted to build one to the point where it was for just to have the computer parts and build it than game on it. The act of building my own PC became more fun than the reason I bought it for. After multiple upgrades, I had done enough PC building and wanted to expand my experience with tech beyond the surface level. That’s when I saw a keyboard that I wanted, but was too expensive. It had a special layout and a TrackPoint like those on ThinkPad laptops. Instead of buying it, I decided to design my own myself. With no experience, I dropped myself in and designed the PCB and case and got it manufactured. In the end, I have a working project that is undeniably mine and has taught me beyond the surface level of electrical engineering. I had successfully been drawn into making electronic devices.

Beyond my interests, I graduated high school in spring 2026 and now attend Georgia Tech as an ECE major, planning on graduating Spring 2029. On campus, I have joined swim club continuing my commitment to swim and made sizeable contributions to the Ramblin’ Competition Rocket club on the electrical team, designing rocket PCBs for real competition rockets. I have found Georgia Tech to be the perfect place for me, with its combination of amazing opportunities and the like-minded people that work towards the same engineering goals.
